Breakdown

Time is measured by the router from traffic to each service's servers, in whole minutes (a minute counts when there was real traffic). A kid's time is summed across their devices, so two devices in the same minute count twice. Category totals count a minute once even if several of its services were active, so services can add up to slightly more than their category.
